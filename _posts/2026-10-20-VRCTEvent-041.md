- `AbstractEventEmitter` and `AbstractSynchronizedEventEmitter`: the internal `_LogInfo`, `_LogWarn` and `_LogError` methods have been renamed to `_LogEmitterInfo`, `_LogEmitterWarn` and `_LogEmitterError`. [`AbstractSynchronizedBehaviour`]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/common/synchronized-behaviour/), the base class of `AbstractSynchronizedEventEmitter`, already defines methods with these names, and Udon# does not support hiding methods
