- Added `StateInitialized` and `OnStateInitialized()` to
  [`AbstractSynchronizedBehaviour`]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/common/synchronized-behaviour/#state-initialization), which
  indicate when the local state has become authoritative (either by synchronizing as owner or by receiving remote state)
- `AbstractSynchronizedBehaviour` now implements `OnDeserialization` - implementations which override it must call
  `base.OnDeserialization()`
- `synchronizationBackoff` is now limited to a minimum of `0.1` seconds within the inspector and invalid values are
  always reported, even when `synchronizationDebugging` is disabled
- Fixed an issue which could cause a pending synchronization retry to be skipped when its timer reached exactly zero
- Fixed [`ArrayUtils.RemoveElement`]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/common/utilities/#arrayutils) - the result no
  longer consists of copies of the removed element
