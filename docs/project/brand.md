- **Icon:** a portal - a circle split down the middle into a white and a purple half, its rings inverting across the
  split.
- **Word mark:** `vrctools_`, always lowercase, in JetBrains Mono ExtraBold. In running text, write **VRCTools**.
- **Variants:** regular files for dark backgrounds, `-light` files for light backgrounds, and the `app-icon` (on a dark
  tile) for photos, busy backgrounds and anything smaller than 24 px.

## Usage

![Do and don't examples for the VRCTools logo]({{ '/assets/brand/guidelines.png' | relative_url }})

**Do** use the files as provided, pick the variant matching the background, keep clear space of at least a quarter of
the icon's height around the logo and scale proportionally.

**Don't** recolor, rotate, stretch, add effects, swap the halves, retype the word mark or rearrange the lockup - and
don't use the logo in a way that suggests VRCTools endorses your project, or that VRCTools is affiliated with
VRChat Inc. or Unity Technologies.

The full guide is included in the press kit as `README.md`.
