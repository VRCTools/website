# vrctools_ press kit

Thanks for featuring VRCTools! This kit contains everything you need to show our logo correctly. Questions or a format
you're missing? Ask on our Discord (<https://discord.gg/FGrt9wv5CF>) or open an issue at
<https://github.com/VRCTools/website>.

## Contents

```text
vrctools-press-kit/
├── README.md            this guide
├── guidelines.png       visual do / don't sheet
├── svg/                 vector logos - use these whenever possible
├── png/                 raster logos at 128, 256 and 512 px
└── social/              1200×630 social preview image
```

| File                          | What it is                                       | Use on                              |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| `logo` / `logo-light`         | Icon + word mark (horizontal lockup)             | dark / light backgrounds            |
| `logo-mark` / `logo-mark-light` | Icon only, transparent background              | dark / light backgrounds            |
| `wordmark` / `wordmark-light` | Word mark only                                   | dark / light backgrounds            |
| `app-icon`                    | Icon on a dark rounded tile                      | any background - avatars, app icons, photos, busy backgrounds |

**Light** variants are for light backgrounds (the left half is ink instead of white). Square PNGs are 128/256/512 px
wide; horizontal PNGs (`logo`, `wordmark`) are 128/256/512 px tall.

## The logo

- **Icon:** a portal - a circle split down the middle into a white and a purple half. Its rings alternate colors and
  invert where they cross the split.
- **Word mark:** `vrctools_`, always lowercase, set in JetBrains Mono ExtraBold - `vrc` in white (or ink), `tools_` in
  purple. The trailing underscore is part of the word mark.
- **Name in text:** in running text write **VRCTools**. The lowercase `vrctools_` form is reserved for the word mark.

## Colors

| Name          | Hex       | RGB              | Use                                             |
|---------------|-----------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Brand purple  | `#8B5CF6` | 139, 92, 246     | Purple half of the icon, `tools_`               |
| White         | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255    | Light half on dark backgrounds                  |
| Ink           | `#0E0B14` | 14, 11, 20       | Light half on light backgrounds, app icon tile  |
| Background    | `#0C0A10` | 12, 10, 16       | Recommended dark background                     |

## Do

- **Use the files as provided.** Prefer SVG; use PNG only where vectors aren't supported.
- **Pick the right variant:** regular variants on dark backgrounds, `-light` variants on light backgrounds, the
  `app-icon` on photos, gradients and anything busy.
- **Leave clear space** around the logo of at least **¼ of the icon's height** on every side.
- **Respect minimum sizes:** icon 24 px, lockup and word mark 24 px tall. For anything smaller (e.g. favicons) use the
  `app-icon`, which stays legible down to 16 px.
- **Scale proportionally.**

## Don't

- Don't recolor the logo or any of its parts, or use colors other than those above.
- Don't rotate, flip, mirror or tilt it.
- Don't stretch, squash or otherwise distort it.
- Don't swap the halves or the ring colors.
- Don't place the regular (white) variant on light backgrounds, or the `-light` variant on dark ones.
- Don't add effects - shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, bevels or textures.
- Don't retype the word mark, change its case or font, or drop the underscore.
- Don't rearrange the lockup or change the spacing between icon and word mark.
- Don't combine the logo with other logos into a new mark, or use it as part of your own product's name or logo.
- Don't use the logo in a way that suggests VRCTools endorses you or your project without our permission.
